# Product

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: private owners of a broken notebook, very often a gaming notebook (Acer Nitro and Predator, Lenovo Legion, Dell Precision) or an older MacBook or iMac. The device does not start, shows no picture or shuts off. They have usually been told elsewhere that the mainboard must be replaced at a price close to a new device. They search by model name or board number, want to know the price before sending anything, and need to trust a workshop they will mail an expensive device to.

Secondary: hobby and trade repairers who buy tested spare parts (displays, display cables, graphics cards for iMac, processors) and workshop supplies by model or part number.

## Product Purpose
Notebook Reparatur 24 is a one-person workshop that repairs notebook mainboards on component level and sells tested spare parts. The website turns a search for a model or symptom into a booked repair at a fixed price, or a diagnosis with a cost estimate, followed by mailing in the device. Success means more repair bookings and inquiries through the own site instead of only through eBay, and a site that is found by search engines.

## Positioning
Component-level board repair instead of board replacement, at a fixed price per model that is visible before the device is sent. Backed by a long eBay track record.

## Operating Context
Customers find the shop through search engines or eBay. The repair runs by mail: book or inquire, ship the notebook with power supply, diagnosis or direct repair, test, return shipping. Repairs carry 1 year of warranty.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Shop with cart and checkout, repair booking as shop item, repair inquiry form, search by model or part number.
- Price examples (state 22.11.2026): mainboard repairs 179 to 249 € per model, diagnosis with cost estimate 29,90 € (whole device) or 59 € (board level, Acer and Lenovo), PS4 maintenance 59,90 €.
- Brands with repair offers: Acer, Apple, Asus, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Medion, MSI, Samsung, Sony.
- The public demo uses sample owner data only (Max Mustermann, Musterstraße 1, 12345 Musterstadt). Real contact data never appears in this repository.
- Demo pages are noindex, no tracking, no third-party requests, fonts self-hosted.
- Planned production system: WooCommerce with Germanized (decision under test).
- Undecided: final logo, final design, payment methods.

## Brand Commitments
Name "Notebook Reparatur 24". No binding logo, colors or fonts yet. Customer-facing language is German, formal "Sie".

## Evidence on Hand
- eBay: 100 % positive ratings, over 4,900 items sold (state 22.11.2026).
- 1 year warranty on repairs.
- Real product photos from the old shop in `assets/bilder/produkte/`, licensed stock photos with attribution in `assets/bilder/fotos/` and `BILDQUELLEN.md`.
- No customer testimonials, no photos of the owner or the real workshop yet. Do not invent them.

## Product Principles
1. The price comes before the shipment. A visitor sees what a repair costs before deciding to send the device.
2. Repair the board, do not replace it. This is the core claim and must be understandable to a layperson.
3. Trust is earned with facts that exist (eBay record, warranty, clear process), never with invented proof.
4. Findable first. Every page is built for search engines and fast mobile loading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 level AA. Many visitors arrive on a phone, often on a second device because the notebook is broken.
